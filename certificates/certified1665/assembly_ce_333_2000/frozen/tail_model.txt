# L11-GT2: nested positive-head transport to infinity

## Status, credit, ownership and snapshot

FOUNDATION, majorant and mollifier-interface certificate, not a new bound on Lambda.
RH dependency: none; no RH statement or height is consumed. The domain is the
real axis y=0. Kornaus PR #180 suggested the collision-exclusion architecture;
the operator's L11 study suggested a complete-head/per-pair-remainder split.
Neither is an input or a citable leg, and this bundle reads no upstream file.

This extends, rather than reclaims, verified-memory snapshot line 595
(dynamics_astra-s4, 598 entries at session start): that fact covered t>=339/2000
and N>=1200000, not the present lower time band. Its covered-graph proof is
reused and its two numerical head regimes are reproduced as anchors. Line 592
is the sign-defect warning: the conservative height loss and the positive
exponent loss in the infinite split are retained here. Line 591 supplies the
context of the exact-phase interface, not an H_t error theorem. The independent
raw error/conditional charge fact at line 597 is discussed only as a future
handoff. No unbanked H5, L7, or other seat's draft is consumed.

The new device is **positive bin-mass transport**. Treat the complete head as a
positive measure on log m. Compute its masses on disjoint integer bins once,
then bound its Laplace transform at every larger saddle by reweighting each bin
at its left endpoint. Use a second, nested complete head at the middle joint.
There is no time-slab enumeration and no sweep over all RS cells.

## 1. Exact quantifiers and definition

Let

    a=333/2000, b=3/16, N1=1500000, h=1499999,
    j=6174242, Z=10^14, P={2,3,5,7,11,13}, D=30030,
    c=500000000001/10^12, e=1/10^24.

For EVERY real t in the CLOSED interval [a,b], EVERY integer N>=h, and EVERY
real x in the LEFT-CLOSED/RIGHT-OPEN cell

    x_N(t)<=x<x_(N+1)(t),  x_N(t)=4*pi*(N^2-t/16),

put

    b_n(t)=exp(t*log(n)^2/4),
    lambda_d(t)=mu(d)*product_(p|d)b_p(t)  for d|D,
    sigma(t,x)=1/2+t*(log(sqrt(1+x^2)/(4*pi))/4-1/(2*(1+x^2))),
    eta(t,x)=t*atan(x)/4-3*t*x/(2*(1+x^2)),
    w=sigma+i*(x/2+eta),
    E(w)=product_(p in P)(1-b_p*p^(-w)),
    q_p=b_p*p^(-sigma), Lambda_1=sum_p q_p*log(p)/(1-q_p),
    Q0=log(x/(4*pi))-4*c*Lambda_1,
    ell0=Q0/(1+2*e),
    c_m=sum_(d|D,d|m,m/d<=N)lambda_d*b_(m/d),
    M=sum_(m>=2)max(1,2*log(m)/ell0-1)*abs(c_m)*m^(-sigma).

The last sum is finite, supported on m<=DN. Both E and the finite Dirichlet sum
use the SAME exact heat-shifted exponent w. The coefficients lambda depend on
t but not x, which is allowed by the resultant's fixed-t differentiation.

The claimed bounds are

    M < 43/50                  for all t,N,x above;
    M < 2/5                    if N>=j;
    M < 1/100                  if N>=Z.

Uniform additional interfaces on the same domain are

    q_p<1, |E|>12/25, |E|<9/5, |dE/dx|<3/5,
    |E_s/E|<=Lambda_1<87/100,
    |w'|<c, 0<eta'<e, Q0>26, ell0>26.

The Q0 and ell0 assertions are about the displayed proxies. **This bundle does
not assert H5, namely 4*Theta'>=log(x/(4*pi)), or that Q0 is an unconditional
lower bound on the actual phase derivative.** Once H5 is supplied separately,
4*(Theta-arg E)'>=Q0 and the exact resultant denominator
4*(Theta-arg E)'/(1+2*eta')>=ell0 follow immediately. See section 8.

The derived integer

    X1=floor(4*pi*(N1^2-3/256))

is output by the checker. For every t in [a,b], x>=X1 implies N(t,x)>=h. Thus
the entire CLOSED rectangle [a,b] x [X1,infinity), y=0, is covered. The small
strip before cell N1 is covered by cell N1-1, not silently assigned to N1.

## 2. Conservative height, time and phase bounds

Define for v>=h

    s(v)=log(v)/2-1/v^2, L(v)=2*log(v)-1/v^2,
    sigma_v(t)=1/2+t*s(v),
    q_p(v,t)=exp(t*log(p)^2/4-sigma_v(t)*log p),
    ell(v,t)=(L(v)-4*c*sum_p q_p(v,t)*log(p)/(1-q_p(v,t)))/(1+2*e).

Direct differentiation gives sigma increasing in x. Let z=t/(16*N^2). The
inequality log(1-z)>=-z/(1-z), pi>3, and x_N>=N^2 give

    log(x/(4*pi)) >= L(N), sigma(t,x)>=sigma_N(t).

Indeed z<=b/(16*h^2), b/(16*(1-z))<1, and the sigma loss is at most
t*[b/(64*N^2*(1-z))+1/(2*N^4)]<t/N^2. All rational end inequalities are
checked at h and strengthen thereafter. This avoids the false exact-start
inequality identified in memory line 592.

Both s and L increase. Since s(h)>log(13)/4, each q_p(v,t) decreases in v and t.
Hence ell(v,t) increases in both variables. In particular ell0>=ell(N,t),
which is >=ell(r,a)>log r for either head r=h or r=j whenever N>=r. All terms
through that head have weight exactly one. The checker also proves
ell(h,a)>26 and L(h)-4*c*Lambda_1(h,a)>26.

The derivatives are exactly

    sigma'=t*x/(4*(1+x^2))+t*x/(1+x^2)^2,
    eta'=t*(7*x^2-5)/(4*(1+x^2)^2).

For x>=h^2 they imply eta'<7*b/(4*h^4)<e and
sigma'<b/(4*h^2)+b/h^6. Therefore
|w'|<=1/2+eta'+|sigma'|<c. These are rational gates, not subtraction of almost
equal floating-point phase terms.

Since q_p<1, the Euler product never vanishes and

    product(1-q_p)<=|E|<=product(1+q_p),
    |E_s/E|<=Lambda_1,
    |E'|<=c*product(1+q_p)*sum_p q_p*log(p)/(1+q_p).

Every expression on the adverse side is monotone in the positive q_p, so the
uniform bounds in section 1 follow by evaluation at (v,t)=(h,a). This proof
covers time-dependent Euler coefficients; no per-slab freezing is needed.

## 3. Complete-head positivity and time transport (banked device replay)

For a complete coefficient beta_m=sum_(d|gcd(m,D))lambda_d*b_(m/d), let
K={p in P:p|m}, k=|K|, r=m/product_(p in K)p, R=log r. On the k vertices place
independent edges {p,q} of rate log(p)*log(q)/2 and a loop at p of rate
R*log(p)/2. An edge of rate rho is present at t with probability 1-exp(-rho*t).
Let C_m(t) be the probability that every vertex is incident to a present edge.
The empty-vertex case has probability one. For a subset S of vertices,

    (product_(p in S)b_p)*b_(m/product S)/b_m
      =exp(-t/2*(R*sum_(p in S)log p
             +sum_({p,q}: {p,q} meets S)log p*log q)).

This is the probability that all vertices of S are uncovered. Exact
inclusion-exclusion gives beta_m=b_m*C_m>=0, including repeated prime powers
(the latter belong to r). This does NOT assert signs of truncated c_m for m>N.

For 0<t1<t2, condition on the present edges at t2. They are independently
retained at t1 with probabilities (1-exp(-rho*t1))/(1-exp(-rho*t2))>=t1/t2.
Any covering configuration has a subcover of at most k edges: choose one
incident edge per vertex. Thus C_m(t1)>=(t1/t2)^k*C_m(t2), yielding
C_m'/C_m<=k/t where C_m>0. The selected primes themselves have C_m identically
zero; all other k>=1 cases have positive cover probability for t>0. For m=6
there is a single positive-rate ordinary edge, so the stronger 1/t bound holds.

For either r=h or r=j, the code proves

    log(m)*(s(r)-log(m)/4) >= k/a

for every applicable 2<=m<=r. Concavity in log m reduces the check to endpoints
[log(m_min),log r] with m_min=4,10,30,210,2310,30030 for k=1,...,6. The missing
case m=6 uses 1/a; the six selected primes contribute zero. For k=0 the
condition is simply s(r)>log(r)/4. Consequently

    beta_m(t)*m^(-sigma_N(t)) <= beta_m(a)*m^(-sigma_N(a))

for all t in [a,b], N>=r, m<=r. This is a quantified comparison, not a time
sample. The complete head of the true M is bounded by the right-hand sum.

## 4. New device: positive bin-mass (Laplace) transfer

For each reference head r in {h,j}, make disjoint closed integer bins [l,u]:
start l=2, set u=min(r,floor(101*l/100)), then replace l by u+1. Let

    mu_[l,u]=sum_(m=l)^u beta_m(a)*m^(-sigma_r(a)) >= 0.

The two numerical lines enclose every such bin mass. For every N>=A>=r put
Delta=sigma_A(a)-sigma_r(a)>=0. Since m^(-Delta)<=l^(-Delta),

    sum_(m<=r,m>=2) beta_m(a)*m^(-sigma_N(a))
       <= sum_bins mu_[l,u]*l^(-Delta) =: Head_r(A).

Using outward upper bin masses gives a certified envelope. This uses only
positivity and monotonicity of an exponential, and works at every real saddle,
not only a sampled one. A constant head would throw away these factors.

The first numerical line obtains each mass by an exact divisor/prefix identity:

    mu_[l,u]=sum_(d|D) lambda_d(a)*d^(-sigma_r(a))
          *sum_(floor((l-1)/d)<n<=floor(u/d))b_n(a)*n^(-sigma_r(a)).

It evaluates every prefix term in Arb and combines all 64 divisor terms. The
second line instead initializes every weighted b_m, multiplies the six Euler
factors by descending prime convolutions, and sums absolute values per bin.
It needs no numerical coefficient-sign decision. Its bin enclosures overlap
the first line's, and it independently proves the final inequalities.

The first pre-registered one-head middle prediction did not pass. The completed
device uses two nested heads: r=h for blocks with A<j, and r=j for blocks with
A>=j. This was explicitly allowed by the original at-most-three-head card,
and the two-head card was written before evaluating the second head. The
failed one-head middle screen is retained as calibration, not asserted as a
negative about the actual M.

## 5. Finite remainder and complete block coverage

For a closed integer-index block A<=N<=B, use its head r<=A and write

    W_AB(t)=max(1,2*(log B+log D)/ell(A,t)-1),
    I_d(A,B,t)=integral_(log floor(r/d))^(log B)
                 exp(t*u^2/4-(sigma_A(t)-1)*u) du,
    Phi_r(A,B,t)=W_AB(t)*sum_(d|D)|lambda_d(t)|*d^(-sigma_A(t))*I_d.

For m<=r the coefficients are complete. For m>r use the triangle inequality
only there, then reindex by (d,n) with dn>r, n<=N. On [1,B],
f(n)=b_n*n^(-sigma_A(t)) decreases because

    t*log(B)/2-sigma_A(t)
       <= b*(log(B/A)/2+1/A^2)-1/2 < 0.

The block ratio B/A is <=21/20+1/h. Therefore the remaining discrete n-sum
is at most the displayed integral, starting at floor(r/d), not r/d and not
ceil(r/d). The floor is positive for every divisor. Hence

    M(t,x) <= Head_r(A)+Phi_r(A,B,t).

All three factors in Phi are nonincreasing in t. The weight decreases because
ell increases. The prefactor's logarithmic time derivative is
sum_(p|d)log(p)^2/4-s(A)*log d<=0. The integrand derivative has sign
u^2/4-s(A)*u<=0, since 0<=u<=log B<4*s(A). The latter inequality is checked
with the BLOCK END and a ratio bound valid for all subsequent blocks; a
pointwise N is not substituted after transport. Thus it suffices to evaluate
Phi at a.

The exact integer chain starts at h and repeatedly takes ceil(21*A/20), clipped
first at j and then at Z. The next block starts at the previous right endpoint.
All blocks include both index endpoints. The first branch is valid even at
N=j; the second branch additionally gives the stronger middle bound there.
The finite/analytic joint N=Z is included by both proofs. On each cell, the
original x range is left-closed/right-open, so every RS transition is included
by the next cell. There is no derivative-of-a-discontinuous-index argument.

The first line encloses each integral using its Arb erfi antiderivative. The
second uses a 64-panel trapezoid upper bound, because for F=exp(t*u^2/4-q*u),

    F''/F=(t*u/2-q)^2+t/2 > 0.

The upper-bound direction is a theorem of convexity, not an empirical
quadrature error estimate. Both lines derive the maximum over the complete
block chain and gate the claimed caps independently.

## 6. Analytic closure for all N>=Z

Let U=log N, epsilon=1/Z^2. Use
sigma>=1/2+t*(U/2-epsilon). The discrete amplitudes on [1,N] still decrease:
t*log(N)/2-sigma<=-1/2+b*epsilon<0. The complete head is bounded by Head_j(Z).
For the weights, replace Lambda_1 by its upper value at (Z,a). The difference

    (2*U-epsilon-4*c*Lambda_1(Z,a))-(1+2*e)*(U+log D)

is increasing in U and positive at log Z, so every weight is one.

For each d put l_d=log floor(j/d). The checker proves 0<l_d<U/2 at U=log Z.
With kappa=3*t*U/8-1/2-t*epsilon, the lower half of its integral is bounded by

    integral_(l_d)^(U/2) exp(t*u^2/4-(sigma-1)*u)du
       <= exp(-kappa*l_d)/kappa.

The term **-t*epsilon** in kappa is essential. Omitting it is exactly the
lower-half sign mistake diagnosed in memory line 592. On [U/2,U], convexity
bounds the integral by

    U/2 * [ exp(-t*U^2/4+U/2+t*epsilon*U)
           +exp(-3*t*U^2/16+U/4+t*epsilon*U/2) ].

Using a sum rather than the maximum is conservative. Kappa is positive and
increases in both t and U. Each displayed endpoint term INCLUDING its factor
U decreases in U at (log Z,a), and the logarithmic derivative only decreases
thereafter. Both terms decrease in t; the code checks those derivative signs.
The prefactors |lambda_d|*d^(-sigma) also decrease in both variables. The
endpoint evaluation therefore bounds all N>=Z, not a grid.

The second line uses a different split, at 2*U/3, with slope
t*U/3-1/2-t*epsilon and upper length U/3. It independently checks the derivative
signs and the infinite cap. The head in both lines is transported from j;
neither line re-enumerates a head at every new height.

## 7. Closed rectangle joint

The checker encloses 4*pi*(N1^2-3/256) strictly between two consecutive integers
X1 and X1+1. It proves

    x_h(a)<X1<x_N1(b).

The left inequality implies X1>x_h(t) for all t>=a; the right inequality says
X1 precedes cell N1 for every t<=b. Therefore x>=X1 belongs to one of the cells
N>=h, including the needed N1-1 strip. The floor cannot be rounded the wrong
way or replaced by an unverified printed decimal. Negative x are not part of
the stated certificate (evenness is an external assembly simplification).

Coverage inventory: t=[333/2000,3/16] closed, N all integers >=1499999,
y=0; near blocks close at j=6174242, middle blocks close at Z=10^14, and the
analytic branch starts at Z inclusively. This proves the M/mollifier rectangle
x=[X1,infinity) only, not an H_t COVER module by itself.

## 8. Handoff, limitations and independent lines

The first-line numerical artifacts are config.json, head_bins.txt,
head_bins2.txt, and certificate.json; the second line regenerates its own head
and remainder tables. Every full check builds from the shipped C sources,
uses one core, and deletes its executables. No outside file read is needed.
The checker reproduces the banked line-595 complete heads at two disparate
reference heights before computing the new heads. The fresh second line has
no import from the first and uses different coefficient and integral formulas.
The symbolic graph replay and independent coverage-DP check audit the old
representation boundary. The new bin boundary has the elementary positivity
proof in section 4 and is independently implemented in both lines. Requested
FOUNDATION ladder: numerical anchors plus a full independent numerical line;
the verifier decides the accepted rung.

H5 and H_t errors are explicitly NOT part of this result. If H5 is provided
separately, then Qphase=4*(Theta-arg E)'>=Q0>26>20 and the exact corrected ell
is at least ell0. Also Emax<9/5<41/20, EprimeMax<3/5<4, support D=30030, and
sum_(m>=2)|c_m|*m^(-sigma)<=M<43/50<1. These are precisely the extra mollifier
hypotheses in memory line 597's conditional charge corollary. That banked
corollary includes the Emax multiplier and all index transitions, but neither
its raw errors nor H5 are recomputed or claimed here. A record package must
cite them as separate accepted facts and use an accepted resultant theorem.
No claim of H_t collision exclusion or a new Lambda bound is made here.

The assembly should import this as a banked claim plus pinned frozen outputs,
not compile C from its confined record script. The included Python-only replay
rechecks exact joins, the maxima, a hash-seeded subset of the finite blocks and
the full analytic-infinity branch. A reduced replay is supplementary; the
claim's full check remains `python3 verify.py`.
